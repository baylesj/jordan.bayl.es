By day I work on the plumbing that lets Chrome play sound. By night I make and play electronic music.

01code

I'm a senior software engineer at Google on the Chrome Media team, working on Chrome's audio stack: the parsers and pipelines that turn audio files and streams into sound. Lately I've focused on hardening that code by moving it to Rust and fuzzing it, and on better debugging tools and docs.

Earlier work includes the backend for tab region capture, a rewrite of Chrome's Cast Streaming implementation, and the Open Screen library.
